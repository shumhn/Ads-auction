What is genuinely working

Base-layer custodyBidder SPL tokens move into a program-owned vault before a live bid is accepted.
MagicBlock hot loopShared auction state and per-bidder budget state are delegated; bids execute through the hosted ER router.
Canonical settlementThe result returns to Solana, creator payout and excess return happen, and a public receipt is written.

P0: make one event work, not a whole marketplace

  1. Create one operator-owned campaign with 9 zones, a beneficiary wallet, campaign terms URI, artwork rules, auction start/end and one-week display commitment.
  2. Make artwork + brand identity mandatory with the bid; add moderator approve/reject and a replacement deadline.
  3. Change outbid economics so losing capital is promptly reusable/refundable instead of locked until the entire event settles.
  4. Add operator controls for soft-close, close/commit, finalize/donate, refunds, retries and no-bid cancellation.
  5. Generate the final 9-zone composite, publish a delivery-proof record, and show payout/refund/donation links on a public results page.
  6. Use real devnet USDC or a clearly labeled event token faucet, then rehearse with 10+ wallets under end-of-auction load.

Recommended event story

“Auction your attention for impact.” A creator or community turns one visible surface into nine sponsorship zones. Brands bid live in stablecoins. MagicBlock keeps the last-minute auction responsive. The winning artwork is composed and published for a promised period. Funds route transparently to the declared recipient, and every winner, refund, placement and payout has public proof.

This is a stronger launch wedge than opening a generic marketplace on day one. Prove one spectacular event, then turn the event tooling into a reusable creator product.
