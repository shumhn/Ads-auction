Non-negotiable event mechanics

  1. One campaign-level USDC budget per bidder, reusable across every spot.
  2. Outbid money becomes available inside the campaign immediately.
  3. Mandatory artwork and exact in-place preview before bidding.
  4. All-or-nothing campaign goal plus explicit no-bid/cancel/refund paths.
  5. Two-minute independent soft close for each spot.
  6. Moderator controls, final composite generation, delivery deadline, and proof-backed payout.
  7. Real public activity and results; no mock claims mixed with chain state.

Why this can win

  • The behaviour is already paid behaviour, not a hypothetical survey response.
  • The visual board is instantly understandable and naturally shareable.
  • Crypto is useful here only where it matters: global backed bids, transparent recipient routing, and settlement.
  • MagicBlock has a visible demo moment: frantic final-minute bidding without repeated base-layer latency.
  • One Drop creates concentrated supply, demand, distribution, and proof before marketplace expansion.
